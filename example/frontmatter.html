Front matter

The block of key: value lines between --- fences at the top of a document is shown as a properties panel. Editing a field rewrites only that property's lines; anything the panel cannot edit (anchors, block scalars, comments) is kept exactly as written. Mod-Alt-Shift-P moves focus to the panel. URL parameters: value, readonly=1, mode=markdown, layout, dir=rtl, theme=dark.
