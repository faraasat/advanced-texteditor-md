Chips

Type @ for people and groups, # for tags (a new one is created on space), ~ for channels and > for commands, in both the Write and the Markdown view. Hover a chip, or put the caret next to it, for its card; Alt ↓ reaches the card's links. URL parameters: mode, value, readonly=1.
