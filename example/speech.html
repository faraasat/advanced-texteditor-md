Dictation and read aloud

Press Mod Shift . (or the microphone) to dictate; interim words appear as grey ghost text at the caret and the final words are typed in. Mod Shift , (or the speaker) reads the selection, or the page from the caret, highlighting each word. Esc stops either. Both depend on the browser: URL parameters: value, mode, lang, commands=1 (spoken "new line", "period"), rtl=1, hl=0 (overlay highlight).
