Reader

The article below is a reader view of a long Markdown text. The outline sticks to the side and marks the section you are in; the thin bar under the top row shows how far you have read. On a narrow screen the outline folds behind the Outline button. URL parameters: theme (light, dark), dir=rtl, depth (outline levels), notes=hide.

An editor with the Reader view command

The toolbar button (or the command palette) opens the text as an article over the page. "Back to editor" or Esc returns.
